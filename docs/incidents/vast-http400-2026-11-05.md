## Fix

`vast_job_runner.refresh_rejected_offer` runs only after the existing 120-second
settling period and two fresh, separated successful absence observations. Only
HTTP 400 before any observed instance qualifies. It refreshes the exact ask; a
still-rentable ask is not retried. Otherwise it refreshes candidates with the
same GPU and no lower RAM/VRAM/CPU/disk/capability, verified status and no higher
compute or transfer prices, plus sufficient remaining host duration.

Durable rejected_offer_ids allows at most three paid attempts total across
restarts; creation_offer carries the refreshed ask into the next guard step.
Budget, deadline and original intent are never extended/rewritten. Stop/cleanup,
unknown/timeout errors, missing price metadata, no safe replacement, and exact
single-job/GPU-replacement authorizations do not rearm. Existing ambiguity and
ownership reconciliation remain intact. No instances rented during repair.
