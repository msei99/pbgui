## Upload recovery

The separate reported upload failure was confirmed in the local supervisor log: uploading began at 20:05:36 and two SSH timeouts exhausted the outer quota by 20:06:13. The fixed attempt quota is replaced by a persisted 15-minute recovery window with 15/30/60-second delays. New observed transfer progress renews it; replaying the same progress does not. The setup timer applies only until worker health is verified. Existing partial-file/chunk resume remains intact, and cancellation, permanent failures and the rental's three-minute collection reserve remain enforced.
