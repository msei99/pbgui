- Fixed #483: Dashboard, Run/Edit, Pareto Explorer and VPS pages use shared mount-aware URL rendering with safe inline initialization. Each view preserves its sort choices through filters, automatic updates and browser reloads while retaining groups, selection and row actions. Restored configuration actions remain disabled until the initial list loads successfully, and unavailable targets are rejected.
