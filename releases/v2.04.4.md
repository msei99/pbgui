# v2.04.4

## Vast.ai rentals

- Rent a selected GPU immediately from Optimizer Settings, before starting queued jobs. Queue start reuses the reserved instance.
- Display the current rental in Settings and Queue, with an End rental action. Manual selections are revalidated and never silently replaced by another offer.
- Keep a manually rented GPU reserved until its first job, explicit release, or rental deadline. Normal idle cleanup applies after use.

## Optimize fixes

- Restore the three default Sweep scoring objectives on graphical Check & Apply for EMA and Trailing, while preserving edited strategy bounds and limits.
- Restore required Long/Short coin-list symmetry and the Sweep starting balance when applying graphical windows.
- Show Save and Save & Queue validation/request failures, indicate pending saves and prevent duplicate submissions.
- Restore new and copied Optimize drafts after refresh in the same browser tab. Closing the editor clears temporary recovery; credential-bearing configs are not stored for recovery.
