# v1.95.16

## Added

- Added a restart-persistent multi-exchange daily-checksum SQLite catalog with scans, repair, unavailable-market cleanup, and reference comparisons for Binance USDM, Bybit, OKX, Bitget, and Hyperliquid crypto.
- Added independent Market Data settings for daily checksum publication to the writable Config/Optimize GitHub archive and anonymous comparison against any configured public archive.

## Fixed

- Made Binance OHLCV builds and integrity repair honor the exact current market `onboardDate`, remove obsolete reused-symbol generations after confirmation, and classify independently verified Binance BTC and Bybit XTZ source-native minute gaps without fabricating data.
- Made Hyperliquid exact-day repair read archived L2Book hours, normalize historical external-fallback H/L envelopes even when minute-gap validation masks the envelope error, and classify unchanged historical minutes as persistent source gaps when they predate both donors or when both exact donor queries complete without returning the remaining candles, while retaining network failures as repairable.
- Made repeated OHLCV Integrity full scans reuse unchanged cataloged files by modification time and size while still detecting additions, deletions, boundary changes, and current-day rollover.
- Displayed the earliest and latest local OHLCV day for each market in the Unavailable Coin Data table.
- Added full-row selection, click-drag ranges, Delete-key removal, and one confirmed restart-persistent Remove Selected/All batch job for unavailable local markets.
- Displayed each PB8 optimize result's configured strategy in a sortable Results table column.
- Displayed each PB8 backtest result's active strategy in a sortable Results table column while keeping pure PB7 views unchanged.
- Made Bybit integrity repair verify a reused symbol's current instrument `launchTime` and automatically remove the complete obsolete local generation before that newer inception, including source-index coverage and checksum rows.
- Kept multiplier-prefixed and unprefixed perpetuals distinct when both exist on the same exchange and quote, migrated legacy `CAT` Market Data selections to `1000CAT`, routed Binance/Bitget downloads and integrity repairs to separate storage, and stopped enriching TradFi/RWA mappings with crypto CMC metadata.
- Changed automatic Bybit refreshes to update only the current UTC day, finalize the previous day once after 00:15 UTC, retry only failed finalizations, and serialize daily file replacements across processes.
- Automatically refreshed OHLCV Integrity summaries and repair rows when background jobs finish instead of requiring a manual Refresh action.
- Displayed every damaged OHLCV day and added a cancellable sequential Repair All job that continues past individual failures.
- Classified damaged Bybit data for removed markets, skipped it during Repair All, and added a confirmed job to remove that coin's complete PBGui OHLCV raw data while preserving PB7/PB8 runtime caches.
- Kept removed Bybit markets exclusively in Unavailable Coin Data instead of duplicating their damaged days in the Repair Queue.
- Preserved quote-like coin suffixes during repair, reconciled independently verified pre-inception days, retried transient batch failures once, and marked Repair All jobs with remaining failures as partial failures.
- Grouped the Repair Queue by coin with aggregated day ranges and reasons, plus a coin-scoped sequential repair action.
- Made integrity scans use stable atomic reads without per-day lock sidecars, invalidate completion before discovery, and serialize inventory deletion against active scans.
- Aligned OHLCV integrity checks with Passivbot's HLCV representation, corrected Hyperliquid fallback candle envelopes, and added a confirmed maintenance job that preserves Open, Close, timestamps, volume, gaps, and source assignments.
- Added coin-scoped and batch integrity repair for every scanned exchange, applied Bybit fallback during initial Hyperliquid builds, and exposed confirmed cleanup of unavailable local markets across all five exchanges.
- Added a per-coin **Details** view with a 24x60 minute-coverage chart and exact gap ranges that distinguish possible inception prefixes from real internal and trailing gaps.
- Excluded leading coverage before the first candle on the earliest local coin day from damaged-minute counts while retaining real internal and trailing gaps.
- Extended gap details with a clickable 15-day context showing hourly coverage for seven days before and after the selected date.
