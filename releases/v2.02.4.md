# v2.02.4

## Added

- PB8 Backtest Results now keep multi-period jobs created together by Optimize Validate in collapsible per-candidate groups.
- Existing PB8 Optimize Suite, Holdout, and Full results without grouping metadata are matched by candidate and strategy identity. Runs with changed strategy or risk settings remain separate.

## Validation And Limitations

- Added isolated regression coverage for validation metadata, historical result inference, collapsible rendering, and selection behavior.
- Final offline release suite: 7,519 passed and 41 skipped.
- Verified read-only against an existing seven-result candidate set containing five Training periods, one Holdout, and one Full timerange; all seven resolved to one group while a changed-risk run remained separate.
- No bot/VPS host was deployed, updated, restarted, or otherwise modified during development.
