# v2.10.14

## AI optimizer research

- Separate optimizer-settings research from the best bot's training result. Keep exact CPU training criteria, qualified trial summaries and downloadable settings recommendations distinct from user-only Holdout and Full Time Range reports.
- Restrict new research experiments to effective Bounds, Limits and Scoring changes, plus explicitly opened strategy/scenario freedoms. Keep the user's per-run work budget and GPU/population/CPU settings fixed. Replicate, Confirm, unchanged repetitions and longer or shorter AI-selected trials are rejected.
- Give each new physical trial fresh controller-owned randomness. The AI cannot set, see, rank or tune seeds. Interrupted physical-job replacements retain their original inputs and seed; exported recommendations use seed=null.
- Plan from the best measured qualified optimizer source with bounded recent feedback. Address relevant pressured bounds in coherent batches and require meaningful scoring exploration after stagnation. Protect hard caps and valid zero/positive parameter floors; outward-adjustment gates check the affected endpoint and tolerate floating-point roundoff.
- Add descriptive parameter-region evidence from the current round's qualified native CPU nominations. Profiles expose selection coverage, ties, incumbent values and requested versus actual work; they are not cross-round pooled statistics or proof of causal improvement.
- Canonicalize dotted and nested Bounds patches before native normalization. Reject overlapping paths and verify the actual executed settings rather than accepting changes that disappear during preparation.

## Faster decisions and reports

- Reuse verified native Vast CPU training results to choose five distinct qualified configurations without duplicate local training backtests. Missing or unverifiable native evidence gets bounded import retries followed by a round-local CPU fallback within the existing validation allowance.
- Replace population-sized AI prompts with bounded summaries, exact paged rankings, compact source-plus-patch proposals and queryable archived evidence. Preserve access to every candidate and exact backend comparison; refreeze obsolete snapshots on resume without rerunning completed searches.

## Vast Stop and recovery

- New proxy-limited Vast Loop jobs install a private, job-scoped watchdog around the existing pinned worker to enforce the fixed quota directly on the remote host. It requests graceful Stop at the first locally reported completed batch reaching the user quota, independently of SSH/provider connectivity. Native batch overshoot remains possible; PB8, the image, seeds and GPU settings are unchanged.
- Persist a non-renewable three-minute retry window for unacknowledged Stop requests, including connection/bootstrap failures and supervisor restarts. Then show **Stop not confirmed**, retain occupied capacity and use status-only recovery probes at most every 30 seconds. Expiry does not authorize automatic rental destruction or replacement; existing **End rental** remains available.
- Distinguish acknowledged delivery from process completion. After the 60-second exit wait, show **Stop acknowledged; waiting for exit** and publish verified partial results within a persisted 180-second transfer/publication window. Retries consume only the remaining window. Partial publication keeps the job nonterminal and does not release the GPU; later proven process exit triggers bounded full collection and replaces the partial result.
- Preserve research metadata, seed, quota and execution verification through GPU replacement. Recover technical validation failures with bounded retries/diagnostic probes inside the existing allowance, reset round-local errors correctly, and handle unavailable reports without repeated paid reassessment loops.
- Keep baseline recovery separate from first-round validation recovery. Resume waits briefly for transient controller-lock contention while preserving cancellation, Stop precedence and ownership checks. Report durations use native completion timestamps rather than delayed collection time.

## Validation and limits

- Release validation follows the mandatory test-gate verdict: focused offline tests for AI Loops, Vast lifecycle/replacement/upload/watchdog paths, Backtest queue/results and affected browser flows. No full-suite run was requested by that gate.
- Validation: 4,715 cases passed in the broad focused run. Three outdated test-call contracts were corrected; the complete affected-area selection then passed all 165 tests, including all three research-download browser cases. The single browser download timeout in the broad run was not reproduced in the exact isolated case or complete matrix. No failing test was excluded. Changed-code inventory has no parse errors; no full repository suite was run, as required by the test-gate verdict.
- Search throughput and reliability improvements do not guarantee a better optimizer setting or a better bot. Holdout/Full Time Range reports remain excluded from AI planning and training decisions.
- This release does not publish a replacement worker image, modify PB7/PB8 bot code, deploy to bot/VPS hosts or add rental/attempt allowances. Restart the API and outdated local Vast supervisors through the existing restart controls to load serial 3664. Already-running physical jobs retain their existing worker path; the remote watchdog is installed for subsequent launches.
