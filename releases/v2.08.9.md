# v2.08.9

## AI Loop optimization

- The AI now selects and explains comparison winners from the user's goals. Removed PBGui's weighted goal score and score-based candidate filtering; complete exact simulations and hard targets remain mandatory. Meeting numeric limits alone no longer ends the search.
- Intentional bot settings and coin overrides survive initialization from the incumbent. HSL, Unstuck, sizing and risk experiments remain adjustable within the user's constraints.
- Added a reviewed handover to replace a Loop on its existing Vast GPU. The transfer stops and collects old jobs, reserves the rental, survives API restarts and preserves spending, budget and deadline without automatically renting another GPU. Fixed false rental-preference mismatches during handover.
- Reduced AI request size with lossless candidate metric tables, configuration differences and shared evidence references. All decision stages compact prior knowledge, preventing oversized round-two selection requests. Runtime failures have clearer messages and bounded retries for retryable errors.
- Fixed comparison archive limits and legacy smoothness metric aliases. Exact liquidation flags are retained as numeric indicators; missing evidence remains unknown and cannot qualify a candidate.
- Comparison views omit instruction-only empty rows and label measurable custom metrics. Approval reviews retain setting names, summarize action effects and collapse long instructions. Transient polling errors clear after reconnection, and Loop log shortcuts follow the active job after technical retries.

## Vast workers and HSL performance

- Vast exports require offline mode and include local candles, market metadata and exchange listing timestamps. Legacy online archives are rebuilt, incompatible snapshots and gap-refetch requests are rejected before rental or dispatch, and workers cannot silently fall back to exchange downloads.
- New jobs, validation and calibration use the published `b9d6692-hsl-cpu-rolling-v1` worker, pinned to `sha256:15698800ffa48634708742425856608c403db26d6aeeb32d6cb5d05b15b0266d`.
- The worker includes GPU HSL history-query caching, adaptive CUDA history-memory sizing and CPU HSL rolling-history reuse with conservative reference fallbacks. Native Auto batching remains enabled instead of being replaced with the population size; explicit batch settings remain authoritative.
- Fixed rental deletion requests and added durable termination status and cleanup errors to GPU cards. Shared provider pacing adapts to rate limits, preserves backoff across unrelated successful requests and spaces deletion retries.
- Updated the English and German AI, Optimize and Vast guides. Previous worker identities remain supported for rental recovery.

The HSL changes are also proposed upstream in Passivbot PRs [#1969](https://github.com/enarjord/passivbot/pull/1969), [#1970](https://github.com/enarjord/passivbot/pull/1970) and [#1971](https://github.com/enarjord/passivbot/pull/1971). Performance varies with candidate history and hardware; unsupported CPU reuse cases retain the reference path.
